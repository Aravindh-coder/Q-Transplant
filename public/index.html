Platform Architecture

Intelligent coordination for Donors, Doctors, Hospitals & Organizer.

Deterministic medical safety rules run first and are never overridden by AI. Grover's quantum search algorithm performs state amplitude amplification across 1,000 donor candidate profiles in $O(\sqrt{N})$ evaluations.

01 · DETERMINISTIC ENGINE

Blood & 6-Locus HLA Match

Checks blood-group compatibility and HLA-A, B, C, DR, DQ 2-digit allele markers before candidate ranking. Medical rules are strictly deterministic.

02 · GROVER QUANTUM SEARCH

O(√N) Amplitude Amplification

Uses quantum oracle phase inversion and diffusion operators to boost matching probability state vector to ~100% in $\lfloor\frac{\pi}{4}\sqrt{N}\rfloor$ iterations.

03 · NIGHT TRANSIT TRACKER

Green Corridor Transit Map

Interactive Leaflet night-themed map displaying Primary Green Corridors, Expressway Bypasses, and Air Helipad transport with live organ ischemia viability clocks.

04 · 1,000 SEEDED DONORS

Real Prototype Database

Contains 1,000 structured medical donor records complete with blood types, organ viability windows, geographic coordinates, and clinical statuses.

05 · ESP32 HARDWARE MESH

Wi-Fi & OLED Alert Console

Wired to ESP32 firmware over a dedicated local Wi-Fi network for real-time OLED hardware alerts and ambulance buzzers.

06 · ORGANIZER AUDIT

Role Access & Audit Logs

Organizer approval workflows for doctors, instant license verification, JWT role-based security, and complete audit logging for every event.

End-to-End Workflow

From Emergency Alert to Successful Transplant Procedure.

01

Verified Onboarding & Registration

Donors complete medical profiles; doctors submit license and credentials for Organizer approval; hospitals pair ESP32 devices.

02

Grover Quantum Matching Search

Doctor runs organ matching. Grover's algorithm evaluates 1,000 candidates with amplitude amplification, outputting top ranked donors with match explanations.

03

Night-Theme Green Corridor Routing

Live organ transit navigation calculates the fastest green corridor path to the hospital, avoiding traffic bottlenecks with real-time ETA countdowns.

04

ESP32 Emergency Broadcast

When urgency becomes critical, ESP32 physical buttons trigger network-wide WebSocket alerts on hospital OLED displays and web consoles within milliseconds.
