The Platform

One coordination layer for donors, doctors, hospitals and the organizer.

Deterministic compatibility rules run first and are never overridden by AI. AI and quantum-inspired search sit on top, as decision support — ranking, explaining, and comparing candidates faster than manual review ever could.

01 · RULES ENGINE

Blood & HLA compatibility

A testable, deterministic service checks blood-group and HLA-marker compatibility before anything else runs. Medical rules are never left to a model to invent.

02 · MATCHING

AI-assisted ranking

Candidates are filtered by organ, eligibility, and urgency, then ranked with a transparent score — every result ships with a plain-language explanation of why.

03 · RESEARCH

Quantum-inspired search

An isolated benchmark module compares classical candidate search against a quantum-inspired optimizer — measuring evaluations, ranking quality, and scalability honestly.

04 · URGENCY

Transparent priority engine

Waiting time, medical urgency and readiness combine into a visible priority score — recipients and doctors can always see why a case was ranked where it was.

05 · HARDWARE

ESP32 emergency mesh

Every partner hospital runs a device with an emergency button, a donor-found button, an OLED display and a buzzer — wired into the same real-time layer as the web app.

06 · GOVERNANCE

Approval & audit trail

Doctors are verified against license and certificate before they can touch a single record. Every approval, match and emergency event is logged.

Registration → Match → Response

What actually happens between a click and a transplant.

01

Verified onboarding

Donors verify by email. Doctors submit license and certificate and wait on organizer approval before the doctor portal unlocks. Hospitals register with verification documents.

02

Compatibility & ranking

A match request runs the deterministic engine — blood group, HLA, organ and urgency — then AI ranks and explains the surviving candidates.

03

Doctor & hospital review

Ranked candidates go to the requesting doctor, then hospital coordination, before anything moves toward a scheduled procedure.

04

Emergency escalation

If a case turns critical, a hospital presses its ESP32 emergency button. Every connected hospital — web dashboard and physical device — is alerted within seconds.

05

Donor found → acknowledged

A responding hospital marks "donor found." The requesting hospital's device and dashboard update instantly, then acknowledges to close the loop.
